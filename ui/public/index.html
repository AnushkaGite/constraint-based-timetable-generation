Amdahl's Law & Tree Search Scaling

In parallel Branch & Bound with root splitting, threads independently evaluate subtrees. Let $S(p)$ be the throughput speedup over $p$ threads: $$S(p) = \frac{\text{Throughput}(p)}{\text{Throughput}(1)}, \quad E(p) = \frac{S(p)}{p}$$ Amdahl's Law models maximum achievable speedup given a parallelizable fraction $f$: $$S(p) = \frac{1}{(1 - f) + \frac{f}{p}} \implies f = \frac{1 - \frac{1}{S(p)}}{1 - \frac{1}{p}}$$
